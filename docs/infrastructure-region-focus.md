# Infrastructure regional view

The Infrastructure map is geographic navigation. Marker positions are approximate and do not encode capacity or project progress.

Selecting Arizona, Texas, Ohio, or Northern Virginia filters the verified event ledger to records explicitly assigned to that region. The view reports current and archived event counts from dated primary-source records. A region with no verified events shows zero records.

The prior demo stage, planned/secured GW, friction score, constraint, and milestone values were removed because no dated source observation supported them. Those fields stay unavailable until a source-specific connector and documented calculation exist. The `All regions` route restores the full event view.

Company research is selected from the shared 20-company registry. The separate [relationship register](relationship-evidence.md) records ORCL–Abilene–ERCOT, CIFR–Apollo–ERCOT and CEG–Crane–PJM; it does not assign every company to a map marker. Apollo's studied-load development and Crane's announced supply agreement are not operating-capacity measurements. PJM demand comes from typed EIA actual-demand observations; geographic proximity does not establish company attribution. Current and archived events retain their own publication and verification dates during partial publisher coverage.
