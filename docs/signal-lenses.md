# Data-center buildout research lenses

Gridline is a focused research workflow for the link between AI/data-center demand, company execution, electric-grid capacity, and market expectations. It deliberately avoids a single buy/sell score. Each lens states its required inputs, calculation or categorical rule, source, observation date, and reason it can be unavailable.

The Overview places these four lenses together in a research brief for any of the 20 tracked companies and the selected region. Regional project and PJM load records are context; they are not assigned to a company without a separately documented relationship. The [relationship register](relationship-evidence.md) records Oracle–Abilene–ERCOT, Cipher–Apollo–ERCOT and Constellation–Crane–PJM connections, preserving each record's development or supply boundary. Other company joins remain unavailable.

| Lens | Research question | Current rule | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market signals | What is the market pricing now? | Latest close versus MA5 and MA10 from ten distinct sourced trading closes; above/below/mixed ordering. | Price behavior is not a forecast or explanation of infrastructure fundamentals. |
| Company financials | What has the issuer reported? | Match diluted EPS or revenue to a comparable prior-year quarter or annual period by tag, unit, duration and filing date; prefer comparable quarters. Show increased/decreased/unchanged only when both exact-source facts exist; otherwise show a disclosure or unavailable state. | Growth direction is withheld when the prior comparison value is non-positive. Half-year/YTD disclosures do not become quarterly growth claims. Reported currency is preserved; adjustments and earnings quality require separate work. |
| Grid demand | Is actual load changing in a tracked grid region? | Mean PJM actual demand for a complete UTC day versus the same weekday seven days earlier, each with 24 typed EIA hours. | Regional load cannot identify data-center demand or confirm that any project has secured power. The card links to EIA's accessible PJM dashboard; per-hour API query URLs remain in observation provenance because key-free API links can return HTTP 403. |
| Project milestones | Which permitting, grid or capacity developments have primary evidence? | Prefer a dated official company development for the selected ticker, then qualifying regional events. Original corporate pages or issuer-bound SEC exhibits must pass identity, title, publication-date and supporting-text validation. | A record's publication does not prove completed capacity, price impact or company-specific exposure to regional load. Retained evidence keeps its own verification date. |

When required source evidence is missing, stale or untyped, the lens displays **unavailable**. Company financials can still show a supported disclosure when a comparable prior period is missing. The Overview summarizes the signal name and method; its source link opens the original dataset or document. Data status shows provider status and event-check scope. The price backtest uses past closes only, enters after the signal day, and excludes pending outcomes from performance statistics. The financial lens keeps the internal `execution` identifier in code while its visible label is “Company financials” / “公司財務”.

## Interview explanation

> “Most stock apps start with a ticker and add broad market indicators. Gridline starts with the data-center buildout question: demand, grid deliverability, issuer execution, and the market response. Each card is backed by dated observations or a specific primary document. If the source cannot support a claim, the card stays unavailable. The signal rules and retrospective test are reproducible, with their limits shown in the UI.”

This is an engineering and research design, not personal financial advice. The current public price feed is a demo provider; commercial use requires suitable data rights and further validation. [SEC CompanyFacts documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and [EIA Open Data](https://www.eia.gov/opendata/) describe the structured source families used here.

The public snapshot does not ingest PJM Data Miner numerical feeds by default. The grid-demand lens uses EIA's public actual-demand data; direct numerical-feed integration requires a separate terms review. PJM's public news discovery is paused, along with ERCOT and Oracle investor news, following access failures. Loudoun, Oracle corporate news and Texas Governor remain the active regional-event providers; [event discovery](event-discovery.md) documents the separate admission and retention rules.
